Case study tool for understanding password creation habits among university students.

Strength: —

Suggested criteria

  • At least 12 characters
  • Includes a lowercase letter
  • Includes an uppercase letter
  • Includes a number
  • Includes a symbol (e.g., !@#$)
  • Avoids spaces

Tips for participants

  • Use a memorable phrase and mix in symbols or numbers.
  • Never reuse passwords across accounts.
  • Consider a password manager for secure storage.

Case study notes

This checker runs locally in the browser and does not transmit or store any passwords. You can use it during interviews or survey sessions to give instant feedback without collecting sensitive data.
